Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or buy Design Your Ideal Day from DTDM (“we,” “us”).

This site is a static page. It does not have accounts, does not ask you to fill in forms, and does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Like any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and keep it secure.

2. When you buy

Checkout is handled by a third-party payment and order platform. It collects what it needs to process your order, such as your name, email address, and payment details, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your full card number. We receive order information, such as your name, email address, and what you bought, so we can deliver the Product and help with support.

3. Your workbook answers

The workbook is a PDF that you fill in on your own device. What you type stays in your file. We do not see it, collect it, or receive it unless you choose to send it to us.

4. Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and provide support. We do not sell your information.

5. How we use information

We use order and contact information to deliver the Product, answer questions, handle payment issues, meet legal and tax obligations, and keep the business running. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

6. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as the hosting, payment, and order platforms, and when the law requires it.

7. Keeping and deleting

We keep order records as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal, tax, and accounting reasons. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. We will respond as the law requires.

8. Children

The Product and site are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

If we add analytics, advertising tools, or email marketing to this site, we will update this policy first. The date at the top shows the latest version.
